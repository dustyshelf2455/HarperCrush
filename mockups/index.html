Glimmerfall · Stage 1

Three looks, three musical feels

Open this on your phone. Tap the board to set off a power. Tap Listen to hear a music sketch; the phone needs one tap before it will make any sound. Nothing here is final. It exists so we can choose a direction.

Each tap on the board sets off the next power in turn: a Comet, a Prism Orb, then a Bloom. After a few seconds without a tap a hint glows on a real valid swap, and the board sets off a power by itself so you can watch the gems fall.

The six gems at real size

Star, heart, drop, leaf, diamond, sunstone. Calm mode uses the first four or five; the sunstone arrives later, in Play mode.

The path

Her companion sits on the current lantern. The other two creatures wait by the path: tap one to pick it, exactly as she will. Companion: .

Music

Three feels for the soundtrack, each composed live in code and never looping exactly. A sketch plays for about a minute. The Wind-down switch shows how it settles in the last minutes of a session. Match chimes on the board are drawn from the chord that is playing, so try tapping the board while a sketch plays.
